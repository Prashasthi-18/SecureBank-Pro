Security, built in

Six layers between your balance and everyone else

Your password is never stored

Only a salted scrypt hash is saved, so a stolen database doesn't reveal it.

Guessing gets you locked out

Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes, and every connection is rate-limited.

Sessions expire on their own

Ten idle minutes signs you out. Signing out revokes every copy of your session, everywhere.

Other sites can't act as you

Every form carries a secret token and cookies are SameSite=Strict, blocking cross-site requests.

You only see your own accounts

Every lookup is scoped to the signed-in owner. Guessing someone else's receipt returns nothing.

Transfers can't half-happen

Debit, credit and ledger entry commit together under a write lock, and a resubmitted form never pays twice.
